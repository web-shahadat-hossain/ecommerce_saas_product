## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

**Product Name**: BazarPro

### Market Size (Bangladesh)

**SME E-commerce Market**:
- Estimated **50,000-100,000** SMEs in Bangladesh selling online
- Growing at **25-30% annually**
- Average spent on tools: ৳2,000-5,000/month

**Potential Market Penetration**:
- Year 1: 500-1,000 subscribers = ৳9-30 lakhs MRR
- Year 2: 3,000-5,000 subscribers = ৳2-3 crore MRR
- Year 3: 10,000-15,000 subscribers = ৳5-10 crore MRR

### Why This Market?

1. **Rising E-commerce**: 18% YoY growth in Bangladesh e-commerce
2. **Mobile-first**: 85% internet users are mobile-only
3. **Payment Infrastructure**: bKash, Nagad, Rocket adoption is increasing
4. **Limited Solutions**: Few affordable, comprehensive platforms for BD market
5. **Untapped SMEs**: Most are still using manual/basic methods
6. **Support Preference**: Strong preference for local support in Bengali
